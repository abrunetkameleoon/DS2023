Introduction

Brand is perception.
Perception is everything.

"

Brand is who people think we are, not who we tell them we are. The goal is for us, our customers, our partners and prospects to share the same understanding of our company. How do we do that? Repeated, consistent, and distinct experiences with our brand.

Brand is created in the minds of consumers. It's the way our company and product are perceived by those who are exposed to it.

This perception is made up of micro- and macro-interactions with our brand: both the highly visible — billboards, website, events — and the more subtle — emails, UX copy, conversations with employees. All of these add up to create our brand perception.

Every Kameleooner who joins becomes an ambassador of our brand.
